Holo-Flux — 3D

Now a true 3D object: perspective projection, depth-sorted particle shell, hex frames spinning in 3D space. Drag the orb to orbit it, scroll to zoom. States still drive the same pipeline — listening pulls the shell in with ripples, thinking swirls it faster in amber, speaking makes the whole swarm shimmer with voice energy, muted dies to grey. All vanilla JS + 2D canvas: no libraries, runs on a laptop CPU.

Agent state

Drag to orbit · scroll to zoom · the orb auto-rotates when you let go. In the real agent: listening = your mic energy, speaking = Kokoro's output energy, thinking = slow internal pulse (no audio). The particle shell is 900 points — trivial for a CPU.
